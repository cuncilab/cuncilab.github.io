Equipment & Facilities

The most relevant equipment necessary to carry out our research activities, available at Dr. Cunci's laboratory at UPR-Río Piedras.

Scanning electron microscope — Thermo Scientific Phenom Pharos

A benchtop scanning electron microscope used for high-resolution imaging of sample surface morphology and nanostructure, essential for characterizing electrode surfaces and nanomaterials.

NX10 AFM/SECM with anti-vibration enclosure (Park Systems)

An atomic force microscope combined with scanning electrochemical microscopy capability, used for nanoscale topographic imaging and simultaneous local electrochemical mapping of surfaces.

Single point Micro Raman Spectrometer, 532 nm

Double TE-cooled spectrophotometer with Olympus CX-43, used to identify chemical composition and structural/vibrational properties of materials at the microscale.

Waveneuro DUO Potentiostat (PINE Research)

With PCIe-6363 NI board and computer. A dual-channel potentiostat/galvanostat integrated with a National Instruments DAQ board for high-speed, customizable electrochemical waveform generation and data acquisition.

Two CH Instruments potentiostats

General-purpose electrochemical workstations used for cyclic voltammetry, chronoamperometry, and other standard electroanalytical techniques.

Two Gamry Reference 600+ Potentiostats and one Gamry Reference 620 Potentiostat, each with computer

Multichannel potentiostat/galvanostat/ZRA systems capable of a wide range of DC and AC electrochemical techniques, including electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS). The Reference 620 is a single-channel variant used for standard electrochemical characterization and impedance measurements.

One Gamry Interface 1010E Potentiostat with computer

A compact, low-current potentiostat/galvanostat with EIS capability, well suited for sensitive or low-signal electrochemical measurements.

Two Sensit BT portable handheld wireless EIS-capable potentiostats, two EmStat Pico wearable EIS-capable bi-potentiostats, and an EmStat 4S Low Range EIS-capable portable potentiostat with computer

Portable, wireless, handheld potentiostats capable of EIS, useful for field or point-of-care electrochemical sensing measurements. The Sensit BT is built around the ultra-small EmStat Pico bi-potentiostat module, which is also used on its own for on-body or miniaturized biosensor applications. The EmStat 4S is a compact, portable, low-current-range variant suited for trace-level electrochemical detection.

Three PINE WaveDriver 200 with EIS Potentiostat with RRDE, each with computer

A potentiostat with EIS paired with a rotating ring-disk electrode system, used to study reaction kinetics and mechanisms at rotating electrodes — especially oxygen reduction reaction studies.

Squidstat Plus Potentiostat with EIS from Admiral Instruments

A general electrochemical workstation for voltammetry and impedance measurements.

Bio-Logic SP-200 Bi-potentiostat with computer

A high-precision bi-potentiostat capable of a broad range of electrochemical and impedance techniques.

Electrochemical Quartz Crystal Microbalance with Impedance Measurement and Dissipation (Gamry)

Tracks nanogram-level mass changes at an electrode surface in real time, combined with impedance/dissipation monitoring for studying film formation, adsorption, and viscoelastic properties.

Fuel Cell Test System — Scribner 850e Multi Range, with Back Pressure module

An automated test station for characterizing fuel cell performance under controlled gas flow, humidity, and temperature conditions.

Epifluorescence Nikon FN1 Microscope with LED excitation X-Cite XLED1

A fluorescence microscope with LED excitation for imaging fluorescently labeled biological or chemical samples.

Multimode Microplate reader — Tecan M200 PRO

Reads absorbance, fluorescence, and luminescence from multiwell plates, used for high-throughput assays.

Synchrotron x-ray techniques via CHESS

Cornell High Energy Synchrotron Source — a shared facility providing synchrotron x-ray techniques for advanced structural characterization, used for studying carbon-supported electrocatalysts.

Two flow cell systems with dual computer-controlled syringe pumps

Electrochemical flow cells used for studying reactions such as water electrolysis under continuous flow conditions.

Two Faraday's cages for low-current experiments

Shielded enclosures that block external electrical noise, used to protect low-current electrochemical measurements from interference.

Type 1 and Type 2 water purification system

Produces ultrapure and general laboratory-grade water for experiments and solution preparation.

1,850 °C high temperature tube furnace

Used for high-temperature synthesis, annealing, or thermal treatment of materials.

X-Carve CNC with computer

A computer-controlled milling machine used for fabricating custom parts, molds, and lab fixtures.

Several 3D printers

Used for rapid prototyping of custom lab equipment, fixtures, and microfluidic device molds.

Dimatix materials inkjet printer

A precision inkjet printing system used to deposit functional inks and materials in custom patterns for fabricating electrodes and other device components.
